How the calculation works

The Eternal Tech bonus is applied to the area's base score, then multiplied by the attacks per hour and the number of marches.

Hourly score = area score × tech bonus × attacks per hour × marches

Important limitations

Actual results may vary because of occupied targets, delayed march returns, disconnections, server conditions, or event rule changes.

Use this as a reference estimate that assumes uninterrupted long-term hunting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Dark Force star level affect the score?

This calculator uses the base score of the hunting area rather than the Dark Force star level.

What is the rotation target count?

For one repeated target, only travel time is used. For two or more targets, the calculator adds travel time to the 240-second base rotation.

Why can the estimate differ from the actual score?

Real gameplay variables such as occupied targets, stopped marches, and network delays are not included.
