How the calculation works

The per-minute scores of all facilities occupied by a server are added together, multiplied by the remaining minutes, and added to the current score.

Projected final score = current score + facility score per minute × minutes remaining

Important limitations

Actual results may vary because of occupation and loss times, battles between servers, or event-rule changes.

This tool is a reference estimate that assumes the current occupation state remains unchanged until the deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should later steps remain visible before they are ready?

Showing every step and using empty or disabled states for unavailable inputs makes the full calculation workflow easier to understand.

How is the projected final score calculated?

The calculator adds the current score to the facility score per minute multiplied by the number of minutes remaining.

Where is my input stored?

Servers, facility assignments, and the end time are stored in this browser's local storage.
